Delete Your Asankary Account

Asankary — Mobile phones & accessories · Iraq — Erbil

This page explains how to delete your Asankary account and what happens to your data. Asankary is published on Google Play and the App Store by Asankary, Erbil, Iraq.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open the Asankary app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

Deletion happens immediately. You are signed out straight away and the account can no longer be used to sign in.

Delete by email

If you cannot access the app, email asankarye@gmail.com from the email address on the account, or send us the phone number you registered with, and ask us to delete your account. We will confirm your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is kept, and for how long

DataWhyRetention
Sales and tax records for completed and delivered orders Required by Iraqi accounting and tax law. These records are kept in our business accounts, separately from your app account, and are not linked to a usable profile once your account is deleted. Up to 7 years
Anonymous crash and diagnostic reports Used only to fix app faults. These contain no name, email or phone number and cannot be traced back to you. Up to 90 days
Encrypted database backups Your deleted data may persist in routine backups until those backups expire and are overwritten. It is not restored or used. Up to 30 days
Store owners: if your account owns a store on Asankary, it cannot be deleted from inside the app, because doing so would erase that store's entire catalogue and every customer's order history with it. Email us and we will handle it as a supervised process.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You can remove some of your data yourself, at any time, without deleting your account:

  • Delivery addresses — Profile → My Addresses, then delete any address.
  • Favourites — tap the heart on a product to remove it.
  • Saved money exchanger details — Profile → Payment Methods.
  • Individual chat messages — press and hold a message you sent.

To remove anything else — a saved payment card, your order history, or a specific set of records — email asankarye@gmail.com from the address on your account and tell us what you want removed. We will action it within 30 days.
