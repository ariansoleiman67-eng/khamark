Delete Your Khamark Account

Khamark — Mobile accessories & electronics · Iraq — Erbil

This page explains how to delete your Khamark account and what happens to your data. Khamark is published on Google Play and the Apple App Store by Khamark, Erbil, Iraq.

Delete from inside the app (Recommended)

  1. Open the Khamark app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the prompt.

Deletion happens immediately. You are signed out straight away, your authentication credentials and profile are permanently removed, and the account can no longer be used.

Delete by email or web request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, email info@khamark.com from the email address registered on the account, or provide the phone number you registered with, requesting account deletion. We will verify your ownership and process the deletion within 30 days.

Note: Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered. If you wish to shop with Khamark again in the future, you will need to create a new account.
