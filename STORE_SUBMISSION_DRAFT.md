## 6. Google Play Data Safety Declarations

- **Data Collected:**
  - *Personal Info:* Name, Email address, Phone number, Physical address (Used for Account management, order fulfillment).
  - *Location:* Approximate and precise location (Used during checkout to select delivery address).
  - *Financial Info:* Purchase history.
  - *Messages:* In-app customer support messages.
  - *Photos & Videos:* Uploaded photos for profile and trade-in inspection.
  - *Device or other IDs:* FCM push notification token.
- **Data Sharing:**
  - No user data is sold to third parties or shared for advertising tracking.
  - Data is only transferred to delivery fulfillment couriers (shipping address, phone).
- **Security Practices:**
  - Data is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS.
  - Users can request data deletion directly within the application (*Profile -> Delete Account*).
