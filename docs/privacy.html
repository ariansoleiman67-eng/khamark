Privacy Policy

Khamark · Last updated: 27 October 2026

Khamark (“we”, “us”) operates the Khamark mobile application, an online store for mobile accessories and consumer electronics in Iraq. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at info@khamark.com.

Information we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work:

  • Account details — name, email address and/or phone number, and your password (stored only in encrypted form by our authentication provider).
  • Profile details you choose to add — profile photo, date of birth, gender, preferred language and currency.
  • Delivery information — shipping address, city/region, and, if you choose to share it, your device location to help fill in an accurate delivery address.
  • Order information — the products you order, quantities, prices, order status and history.
  • Messages — messages you send to the store through in-app chat.
  • Device notification token — an identifier used solely to deliver push notifications about your orders and offers.
  • Basic usage data — app version and language, used to operate and improve the app.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To process, deliver and support your orders.
  • To send order updates and, if you allow notifications, offers.
  • To answer your messages and provide customer support.
  • To detect abuse and keep the service safe.

We do not sell your personal information.

Services we use

  • Supabase — database and authentication hosting, with encrypted storage in the cloud.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — delivers order and promotional notifications to your phone.

Permissions the app may request

  • Camera — used to scan barcodes or take a photo of your device when submitting a trade-in request or uploading a profile image.
  • Photo Library — used so you can attach an existing photo to a trade-in or chat message.
  • Location (when in use) — used solely to help you pick your delivery address on the map during checkout.
  • Notifications — used to keep you informed about order progress and exclusive offers. You can turn this off at any time in your phone’s system settings or the in-app profile.

Your rights and account deletion

You have the right to access, update, or permanently delete your account at any time:

  • Inside the app: Go to Profile → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.
  • Via web or email: See our Account Deletion Guide or email info@khamark.com.
