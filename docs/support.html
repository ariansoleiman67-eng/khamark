Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I place an order? Browse products by category or brand, select your variations (color/storage), tap Add to Cart, and proceed to checkout. Payment is Cash on Delivery.
  • How do I track my delivery? Open the Orders tab to view real-time delivery status for all active and completed orders.
  • What areas do you deliver to? We offer express same-day courier delivery across Erbil, and reliable standard shipping to Sulaymaniyah, Duhok, Baghdad, and all Iraqi governorates.
  • How do second-hand trade-ins work? Go to Trade-In in the app, select your device, answer a few condition questions, and attach photos. Our technicians will provide a transparent cash or store credit offer within 24 hours.
  • How do I delete my account? Go to Profile → Delete Account in the app, or read our Account Deletion Guide.
