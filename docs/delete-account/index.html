Delete Your Khamark Account

Khamark — Mobile accessories & electronics · Iraq — Erbil

This page explains how to delete your Khamark account and what happens to your data. Khamark is published on Google Play and the Apple App Store by Khamark, Erbil, Iraq.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app (Immediate)

  1. Open the Khamark app on your device and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the screen and tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation alert and tap Delete Forever.

Account deletion happens immediately. You are signed out straight away, your authentication credentials and profile information are permanently removed, and your account cannot be restored.

Option 2: Delete by web or email request

If you have uninstalled the app or do not have access to your mobile device, you can submit a manual deletion request:

Email info@khamark.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Please include your registered email address or phone number. We will verify your identity and process the deletion within 30 days.

Note: Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered. If you wish to shop with Khamark again in the future, you will need to create a new account.
